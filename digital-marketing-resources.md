# Digital Marketing Resources for French Businesses

A curated list of tools, guides, and publications for digital marketing professionals and French business owners.

## News & Analysis

- [Magazine Digital](https://magazine-digital.fr) — French digital marketing magazine covering SEO, social media, AI tools, and digital transformation for SMEs
- [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com) — English-language search marketing news
- [Journal du Net](https://journaldunet.com) — French business and digital news

## SEO Tools

- **Google Search Console** — Free, essential for indexing and performance monitoring
- **Screaming Frog** — Site crawl and technical SEO audits
- **Semrush / Ahrefs** — Keyword research and competitive analysis

## Content Marketing

- **Notion / Obsidian** — Editorial planning and knowledge management
- **BuzzSumo** — Content performance and influencer discovery
- **Canva** — Visual content creation for non-designers

## Social Media Management

- **Buffer / Hootsuite** — Multi-platform scheduling
- **Metricool** — Analytics and scheduling (popular in France and Spain)

## AI Writing Assistants

- **Jasper / Copy.ai** — English-first AI writing tools
- **Writesonic** — Multilingual AI content generation
- Claude (Anthropic) — Long-form research and analysis

## Learning Resources

- Google Digital Garage — Free digital marketing certification
- HubSpot Academy — Inbound marketing, CRM, and content courses
- OpenClassrooms — French-language digital marketing courses
