# French Workplace Equality Index (Index Egapro) — Resources

A curated list of resources for French employers required to calculate and publish the Index Egalite Professionnelle.

## What is the Index Egapro?

The Index Egalite Professionnelle (Egapro) is a mandatory gender pay gap tool for French companies with 50+ employees.
Created by the Loi Avenir Professionnel (2018), published annually by March 1.

## Scoring (max 100 points)

Companies 250+ employees (5 indicators):
- Pay gap by role category: 40 pts
- Individual pay increase differences: 20 pts
- Promotion rate differences: 15 pts
- Post-maternity leave pay raises: 15 pts
- Women in top 10 earners: 10 pts

Companies 50-249 employees: 4 indicators, adjusted weighting.

## Key Deadlines

- Feb 28: Calculate index on previous year data
- Mar 1: Publish on company website + declare on Egapro platform
- Mar 31: Submit to DREETS if score < 85

## Penalties

Below 75/100 for 3+ years: up to 1% of payroll. Below 85: action plan must be published.
