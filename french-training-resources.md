# French Professional Training Resources

A curated list of resources for professional training and certification in France.

## Official Certifications & Directories

- [Trouver-Formation.fr](https://trouver-formation.fr) — Search engine for Qualiopi-certified training organizations in France. Filter by sector, location, funding (CPF, OPCO), and certification type (RNCP/RS).
- [Mon Compte Formation](https://www.moncompteformation.gouv.fr) — Official French government CPF portal for booking and funding training
- [France Compétences](https://www.francecompetences.fr) — National body overseeing qualification frameworks (RNCP, RS)

## Qualiopi Certification

Qualiopi is France's mandatory quality certification for training organizations accessing public funding (CPF, OPCO, France Travail). As of 2026, over 80,000 organizations are certified.

Key resources:
- Official quality reference framework (Référentiel National Qualité)
- List of accredited certification bodies (COFRAC-accredited)
- Audit indicators for initial certification and surveillance

## CPF (Compte Personnel de Formation)

Every French worker accumulates CPF credits (€500/year, capped at €5,000; €800/year for workers without a bac, capped at €8,000). These credits fund:
- Certified vocational training (RNCP/RS)
- Language certifications (TOEIC, TOEFL, DELF)
- Digital skills (PCIE, MOS)
- Driving license (category B)

## Funding Bodies (OPCOs)

France has 11 OPCOs (Opérateurs de Compétences) that finance training for employers:
- AFDAS (culture, media, communication)
- ATLAS (finance, consulting, management)
- CONSTRUCTYS (construction)
- OPCO 2i (industry)
- EP (liberal professions)
- And 6 others by sector
