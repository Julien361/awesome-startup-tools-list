## Framework: How Startups Should Evaluate Software

### Stage 1 — Pre-seed to Seed
At this stage, prioritize **free tiers and open-source** options. Avoid annual contracts. Focus on:
- Freemium tools with generous limits (Notion, Linear, Figma)
- Open-source self-hosted (Metabase for BI, n8n for automation)
- Pay-as-you-go APIs (Stripe, Twilio, SendGrid)

### Stage 2 — Series A to B
At growth stage, evaluate tools on **scalability and integrations**:
- Does it have an API? Can you export your data?
- Native integrations or Zapier dependency?
- SLA guarantees and support tiers?
- Compliance certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR)

### Stage 3 — Scale-up
Focus shifts to **total cost of ownership** and vendor consolidation:
- Negotiate enterprise contracts with usage caps
- Evaluate build vs buy for core capabilities
- Vendor diversification to avoid single points of failure

## Must-Have Categories for SaaS Startups

- **CRM**: HubSpot / Pipedrive / Salesforce (by company size)
- **Support**: Intercom / Zendesk / Crisp
- **Analytics**: Mixpanel / Amplitude / PostHog (open-source)
- **Error tracking**: Sentry / Datadog
- **Auth**: Auth0 / Clerk / Supabase Auth
- **Payments**: Stripe / Paddle (with tax management)
- **Communications**: SendGrid / Postmark / Brevo
