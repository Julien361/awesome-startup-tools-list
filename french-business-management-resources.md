# French Business Management Resources

A curated list of resources for French entrepreneurs, business owners, and managers navigating the French business landscape.

## Legal & Regulatory

- [Légifrance](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr) - Official French legal database, laws and regulations
- [INPI](https://www.inpi.fr) - French patent and trademark office, company registration
- [BPI France](https://www.bpifrance.fr) - Public investment bank, grants and financing for SMEs

## Business News & Analysis

- [Revue Entreprise](https://revue-entreprise.fr) - In-depth articles on French business law, HR, management, and strategy for entrepreneurs and SME managers
- [Les Echos](https://www.lesechos.fr) - Leading French business daily
- [La Tribune](https://www.latribune.fr) - French business and financial news

## HR & Employment Law

- French Labor Code (Code du Travail) - Governs employment contracts, collective agreements, working conditions
- Key concepts: CDI/CDD contracts, rupture conventionnelle, collective bargaining, URSSAF contributions

## Accounting & Finance

- Plan Comptable Général (PCG) - French chart of accounts
- Liasse fiscale - Annual tax return for companies
- Expert-comptable - French chartered accountant, mandatory for companies above certain thresholds

## Company Formation

- SAS (Société par Actions Simplifiée) - Most popular structure for startups, flexible governance
- SASU - Single-shareholder SAS, common for solo founders
- EURL - Single-member LLC equivalent

## Government Programs

- France 2030 - Investment plan for industrial and technological sovereignty
- Crédit d'Impôt Recherche (CIR) - R&D tax credit, up to 30% of eligible expenses
- French Tech - Government initiative supporting tech startups
