# HR Compliance Tools for German Companies in 2026

A curated list of resources and tools for HR and legal compliance in Germany, with a focus on transparency and reporting obligations.

## Wage Transparency (Entgelttransparenz)

The *Entgelttransparenzgesetz* (EntgTranspG) requires German employers to provide information about pay structures and enables employees to request pay comparisons. In 2026, stricter enforcement and upcoming EU Pay Transparency Directive implementation are increasing compliance pressure.

**Key resources:**
- [Entgelttransparenz-Guide.de](https://entgelttransparenz-guide.de) — Plain-language guide to German wage transparency obligations, compliance checklists, and FAQ for HR teams
- [Bundesamt für Justiz EntgTranspG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/entgtranspg/) — Official legal text
- EU Pay Transparency Directive 2023/970 — Must be transposed by June 2026

## Works Council Obligations (Betriebsrat)

- Information and consultation requirements under BetrVG §87 for HR decisions
- Required documentation for headcount changes, working time modifications, and remote work policies

## German Employment Documentation

- Employment contracts (Arbeitsvertrag): written form required for fixed-term contracts
- Certificates of employment (Arbeitszeugnis): mandatory upon request
- GDPR-compliant data processing agreements for HR software (AV-Vertrag)

## Payroll Compliance Tools

- DATEV Lohn und Gehalt — leading German payroll software
- Sage HR — mid-market HR platform with German localization
- Personio — popular HR software for German SMEs (compliance module included)

## EU Pay Transparency Directive: What Changes in 2026

Companies with 250+ employees must report gender pay gap data starting June 2026 under the EU directive. Key requirements:
- Annual reporting obligation for companies with 250+ employees
- Right to pay information before and during employment
- Burden of proof shifts to the employer in equal pay disputes
