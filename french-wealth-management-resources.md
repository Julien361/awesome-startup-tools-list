# French Wealth Management & Patrimoine Resources

A curated list of French-language resources for wealth management, patrimoine planning, and personal finance.

## Magazines & Publications

- [Revue Patrimoine](https://revue-patrimoine.fr) — French magazine covering wealth management, investment strategies, tax optimization and estate planning for individuals and professionals
- [Revue Entreprise](https://revue-entreprise.fr) — Business management and corporate strategy publication

## Key Topics Covered

### Wealth Planning
- Assurance-vie (life insurance as investment vehicle)
- PEA (Plan d'Épargne en Actions) — tax-advantaged stock accounts
- Immobilier (real estate investment and SCPI)
- Succession et donation (inheritance and gift planning)

### Tax Optimization
- Impôt sur la fortune immobilière (IFI)
- Défiscalisation loi Pinel/Denormandie
- Réduction IR via investissements

### Retirement (Retraite)
- PER (Plan d'Épargne Retraite) — new pension savings plan
- Complémentaire retraite MADELIN for self-employed
- Lissage des revenus en fin de carrière

## Tools & Calculators

- Simulateur impôt succession — calculate inheritance tax
- Calcul rendement SCPI — REITs yield calculator
- Comparateur assurance-vie — life insurance comparison

## Regulatory Framework

- Directive MIF2 (MiFID2) — investor protection rules
- Règlement PRIIPS — packaged investment products
- AMF regulations — French financial markets authority
