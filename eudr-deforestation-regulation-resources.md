## What is EUDR?

The EU Deforestation Regulation prohibits placing certain commodities on the EU market if linked to deforestation. Applies to:
- Cattle, cocoa, coffee, palm oil, soya, wood, rubber
- Derived products (leather, chocolate, furniture, paper, tyres)

**Key deadlines:**
- Large companies: December 30, 2025
- SMEs: June 30, 2026

## Due Diligence Requirements

Operators must:
1. Collect geolocation data for all production plots
2. Assess country/area risk classification (low/standard/high)
3. Submit due diligence statements via the EU Information System
4. Maintain documentation for 5 years

## Country Risk Classification

The EU classifies countries as low, standard, or high risk:
- Low risk: simplified due diligence
- Standard risk: full due diligence
- High risk: enhanced due diligence
