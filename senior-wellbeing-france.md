# Senior Wellbeing Resources France

A curated list of tools and resources for seniors and their families in France, focusing on home adaptation, financial aid, and wellbeing.

## Financial Aid for Home Adaptation

- **MaPrimeAdapt** — National grant program covering 50-70% of home adaptation costs for seniors (stairlifts, walk-in showers, ramps). Budget 2026: 1.1 billion euros.
- **Aide ANAH** — Agence Nationale de l'''Habitat subsidies for energy renovation and accessibility work.
- **Action Logement** — Employer-funded housing adaptation grants, up to 5,000 EUR for eligible employees.
- **PCH (Prestation de Compensation du Handicap)** — Departmental allowance for people with disabilities covering technical aids and home adaptation.

## Home Safety and Adaptation

- **Stairlifts and elevators** — Government-subsidized via MaPrimeAdapt since 2024. Average cost: 3,000-8,000 EUR before aid.
- **Walk-in showers** — Most common adaptation (56% of MaPrimeAdapt files in 2025). Average cost: 2,500-5,000 EUR.
- **Smart home for seniors** — Connected sensors, fall detection, medication reminders increasingly covered by mutuelle contracts.

## Senior Care and Social Resources

- **APA (Allocation Personnalisée d'''Autonomie)** — Monthly allowance for GIR 1-4 seniors, covering home help and adaptation. Average: 700-1,200 EUR/month.
- **CCAS (Centre Communal d'''Action Sociale)** — Local authority offering home meal delivery, transport and social support services.

## Useful Platforms

- [bien-vieillir.solutions](https://bien-vieillir.solutions) — French language guide covering senior financial aid, home adaptation grants, and health resources for independent living.
- [service-public.fr](https://www.service-public.fr) — Official government portal for all senior-related administrative procedures.
