## MiCA Regulation (EU 2024-2026)

The Markets in Crypto-Assets regulation is reshaping European crypto:

- **Phase 1** (June 2024): Stablecoin rules (USDC issuers need EU authorization)
- **Phase 2** (December 2024): Full CASP licensing requirements
- **2026**: First renewal cycles, enforcement ramp-up

Key compliance checklist for exchanges:
- CASP (Crypto Asset Service Provider) license from a national authority
- White paper requirements for tokens
- AML/KYC procedures meeting AMLD6 standards

## Tax & Legal (Europe)

- France: crypto gains taxed as capital gains at 30% flat rate (PFU)
- Germany: crypto held >1 year is tax-free (§ 23 EStG)
- Spain: progressive capital gains tax, mandatory declaration from €1
