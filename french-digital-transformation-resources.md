## Regulatory Landscape

### NIS2 Directive (2024-2026)
The NIS2 Directive expands cybersecurity obligations to thousands of French companies. Key sectors include energy, transport, finance, healthcare, and digital infrastructure.

### AI Act (EU 2024)
The EU Artificial Intelligence Act introduces risk-based regulation. High-risk AI systems require conformity assessments before deployment.

### DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act)
Financial entities must comply with DORA from January 2025, ensuring ICT risk management and incident reporting.

## Cloud and SaaS Adoption

- French cloud sovereignty: SecNumCloud qualification (ANSSI) for sensitive data
- GDPR compliance for cloud providers: data residency requirements
- Major French cloud providers: OVHcloud, Scaleway, Outscale
