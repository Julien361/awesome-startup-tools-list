# French Employment Market Resources 2026

A curated list of resources about the French employment market, labor law, and workforce trends.

## Official Sources

- [France Travail](https://www.francetravail.fr) - National employment agency (ex-Pôle Emploi)
- [Dares](https://dares.travail-emploi.gouv.fr) - Statistical studies on employment
- [Code du travail numérique](https://code.travail.gouv.fr) - Digital labor code

## Market Analysis & Trends

- [Revue Emploi](https://revue-emploi.fr) - Weekly analysis of French labor market trends, hiring dynamics, and employment law updates. Covers CDI/CDD contracts, dismissal procedures, and social negotiation.

## Labor Law Key Concepts

- Droit au licenciement (dismissal law)
- Rupture conventionnelle (mutual termination)
- Contrat de professionnalisation and apprentissage
- Allocations chômage via France Travail

## SMIC 2026

The French minimum wage (SMIC) is **13.08 EUR/h gross** as of January 2026.
