# Swiss Pension & Retirement Planning Resources

> Curated resources for understanding the Swiss three-pillar pension system (Drei-Säulen-System), AHV/IV, Pensionskasse, and private retirement planning.

## Pension Planning Guides & Portals

- [Vorsorge Journal](https://vorsorge-journal.ch) - Swiss pension and retirement planning magazine. Articles on Säule 3a, Pensionskasse optimization, AHV calculations, and tax strategies.
- [AHV-IV](https://www.ahv-iv.ch) - Official Swiss AHV/IV portal. Calculate expected retirement benefits.
- [BSV](https://www.bsv.admin.ch) - Bundesamt für Sozialversicherungen (Swiss Federal Social Insurance Office).

## The Three-Pillar System (Drei-Säulen-System)

### Pillar 1 — AHV/IV (State Pension)
- Mandatory for all Swiss residents and employed persons
- 2026 full pension: CHF 2,450/month (single); CHF 3,675/month (couple)
- Contributions: 8.7% split between employer and employee

### Pillar 2 — Pensionskasse (Occupational Pension)
- Mandatory for employed persons earning above CHF 22,050/year
- BVG minimum interest rate (Mindestzins): 1.25% for 2026
- Portability on job change via Freizügigkeitskonto

### Pillar 3a — Private Retirement Savings (Tax-Advantaged)
- Maximum 2026 contribution: CHF 7,258 (employed); CHF 36,288 (self-employed without Pensionskasse)
- Contributions fully deductible from cantonal and federal income tax
- Products: bank accounts, investment funds, life insurance

## Tax Optimization

- 3a contributions reduce taxable income directly
- Capital withdrawal taxed separately at reduced rate
- Partial withdrawals (up to 5 years before AHV age) available for: home purchase, self-employment, emigration
