# French Employment Law Resources 2026

A curated list of resources for understanding French employment law in 2026.

## Official Resources

- [Legifrance](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr) - Official French legal database
- [Ministère du Travail](https://www.travail-emploi.gouv.fr) - Ministry of Labor
- [Conseil des prud'hommes](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F205) - Labor courts guide

## Key Concepts for Employers

- **CDI vs CDD**: Permanent vs fixed-term contracts
- **Rupture conventionnelle**: Mutual termination agreement
- **Indemnité de licenciement**: Severance pay calculation
- **CSE**: Works council obligations (11+ employees)

## Employment Law News & Analysis

- [Revue Emploi](https://revue-emploi.fr) - Updated French employment law resources and news 2026

## Severance & Termination

- [Calcul Indemnité Licenciement](https://indemnite-licenciement.fr) - Severance pay calculator

## Worked Examples

- Periode d'essai: 2 months (workers/employees), 3 months (technicians), 4 months (executives)
- Rupture conventionnelle: minimum 1/4 month per year (up to 10 years), then 1/3
- Preavis: 1 month (less than 2 years), 2 months (2+ years) for non-executives
