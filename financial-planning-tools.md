# Financial Planning & Cost Estimation Tools for Startups

> A curated collection of free calculators and financial planning tools for entrepreneurs

## Business Cost Estimators

### Startup Cost Calculators
- **[HowMuchToStartABusiness.com](https://howmuchtostartabusiness.com)** — Free startup cost calculator by industry. Estimates one-time costs (equipment, licenses, deposits) and recurring costs (rent, salaries, subscriptions) based on your business type and location. Covers 50+ business types.
- **Bplans Startup Cost Calculator** — Template-based with industry benchmarks
- **Shopify Business Name Generator + Cost Estimator** — Useful for e-commerce ventures

## Financial Projections

### Revenue & Profitability
- Break-even analysis calculators
- Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) estimators
- Unit economics calculators (CAC, LTV/CAC ratio)

### Funding Requirements
- Runway calculators (given burn rate and cash on hand)
- Dilution calculators for equity rounds
- Convertible note calculators

## Legal & Compliance Costs

Understanding regulatory costs upfront saves surprises later:
- Business registration fees vary widely by state (50-500+ USD)
- LLC formation costs differ from C-Corp costs (important for VC-backed startups)
- Industry-specific licensing (food service, healthcare, finance) adds significant overhead

## Operational Cost Tools

- **Salary benchmarking**: Glassdoor, Levels.fyi for tech roles
- **Office space calculators**: SquareFoot, Hubble for flexible workspace costs
- **SaaS stack cost estimators**: Track recurring software subscriptions before they spiral
